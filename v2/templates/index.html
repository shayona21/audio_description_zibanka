How each line is made

  1. Voice: ElevenLabs reads TARGET DIALOGUE with the row's VOICE ID (Voice Library). Silence before the voice is cut, so speech starts on TCR IN.
  2. Rewrite: if a line would need faster than 1.2x or slower than 0.8x to fit, Gemini rewrites it with the same meaning, tone and character, and it is voiced again (up to 2 tries). Lines of one or two words are not rewritten: they stay at 1.0x, marked SKIPPED_REWRITE.
  3. Speed: lines more than 2% off are sped up or slowed down, 0.8x–1.2x, pitch kept. Lines that still don't fit stay at 1.0x and are marked MANUAL_REVIEW.

In the ZIP

  • pass2/tracks/: one WAV per episode and character, the final audio
  • pass2/lines/: each line as delivered (rewritten and sped up where needed)
  • pass1/lines/: each line as ElevenLabs first voiced it, before any rewrite or speed change
  • processing_report.csv: per line timings, speed, rewrite and status; plus a timeline CSV per episode and the script

A line starts on its TCR IN, or straight after the same character's previous line if that one runs late. Open the ZIP in the Dubbing Quality Check to review it.
